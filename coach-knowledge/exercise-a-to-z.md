# Exercise A to Z

Reference list the coach uses to recommend exercises when a client gives feedback (equipment, pain, goal, level). It is not every exercise that exists, and the coach can suggest others. General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Sharp pain means stop that exercise.

**Level:** B beginner, I intermediate, A advanced. Keep exact names when saving to a plan, because logs match on the name. Equipment is per client: swap in what they have and prefer. Single-leg moves start with dumbbells and move to a barbell or Smith machine once outgrown.

## Find by need
| Client needs | Look at |
| --- | --- |
| Upper-glute shelf | Hip abduction, side-lying abduction, banded cha-cha, top-of-bridge abduction, banded bridge, hip hike |
| Lower glute and stretch | Squats, lunges, step-ups, RDL, deficit reverse lunge, Bulgarian split squat |
| Low quad with glutes | Frog pump, barbell bridge, American hip thrust, reverse hyper, 45-degree back extension |
| Hamstrings | Leg curls, sliding curls, Nordics, glute ham raise, RDL, straight-leg bridge |
| Bad back | Hex-bar or sumo deadlift, front squat, belt squat, American hip thrust, bridges, landmine row |
| Bad knees | Reverse lunge, step-up, hip-dominant lunge (long stride), thrusts and bridges, box squat |
| Only dumbbells | Goblet squat, dumbbell Bulgarian split squat, dumbbell RDL, dumbbell hip thrust, single-leg thrust |
| No equipment | Bridge ladder, B-stance and shoulder-elevated thrust, Cossack, skater, pike push-up, towel row |
| Upper chest | Incline barbell or dumbbell press, feet-elevated push-up |
| Rear and side delts | Rear delt fly, face pull, lateral raise (cable or dumbbell) |
| Slim waist | Crunch, dead bug, plank, cable crunch, no heavy side bends |
| Cannot feel glutes | Posterior pelvic tilt hold, standing glute squeeze, glute march, quadruped extension |
| Low-soreness volume | Quadruped kickbacks, step-ups, sled push, cable kickbacks |
